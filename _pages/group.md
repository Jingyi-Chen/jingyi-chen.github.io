## Our Group

Welcome to our research group! We explore atmospheric processes through observations, numerical modeling, and theoretical analysis, with a particular focus on clouds, convection, and their interactions with the environment.



## Mentoring Philosophy

My goal as a mentor is to support both your **professional and personal growth**, helping you develop essential skills in identifying opportunities, asking meaningful questions, solving problems, and communicating effectively.

I believe that research is about more than *"publish or perish."* Beyond academic achievements, I encourage you to appreciate the broader impact of your work, develop intellectual curiosity, and discover your own motivation.

**Happiness, a sense of achievement, and self-empowerment** are essential to sustained growth. I hope our collaboration will be a rewarding and enjoyable journey, helping you develop from a student into an independent researcher or a confident professional in your chosen career.
